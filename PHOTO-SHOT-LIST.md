# H3O — Photo Shot List

Real photos are the single biggest upgrade left. You don't need a photographer — a phone in **daylight** beats studio gear in bad light. Shoot these, send them over, and I'll place them in the site.

## Setup (2 minutes)
- **Light:** near a big window, in open shade outside, or morning/late-afternoon sun. Avoid harsh noon glare and indoor yellow light.
- **Background:** keep it simple — a wood table, a clean cooler lid, grass, or a plain wall. Clutter kills the "premium" feel.
- **Phone:** wipe the lens, tap the screen to focus on the bottle, hold steady. Shoot a few of each so you can pick.

## The shots (in priority order)
1. **Hero bottle — Seeded Watermelon.** One bottle, straight on, filling most of the frame. Condensation/water beads = looks fresh. → *becomes the top of the site.*
2. **Hero bottle — Mango Pineapple Orange.** Same treatment. → *the second flavor card.*
3. **Both bottles together.** Side by side, slight angle. → *"Our Juices" section.*
4. **The pour or the press.** Watermelon being pressed, or juice pouring into a bottle. Action = story. → *"hand-pressed" proof.*
5. **The real setup.** Your truck + coolers, or bottles on ice in the cooler. Authentic, not staged. → *"Our Story" section.*
6. **You / the hands.** Hands holding a fresh bottle, or you at a market. People trust faces and hands. → *story + social.*
7. **Overhead flat-lay.** Bottles + cut fruit (watermelon wedge, mango, pineapple) arranged on a surface, shot from directly above. → *great for Instagram too.*

## For social (@h3ojuicefl) while you're at it
- A few **vertical** (portrait) versions of the above — Instagram/TikTok want tall, not wide.
- A short **video clip** of the pour — even 5 seconds. Motion sells juice.

## Send me
Just text/AirDrop the photos to yourself and drop them in — I'll crop, color-balance if needed, and wire them into the site and the social-ready sizes. No editing skills required on your end.
