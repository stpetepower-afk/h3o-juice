## Visual language (match the site)

- **Palette:** Deep near-black greens, soft green accent (`#7cb342` / `#9ccc65`), warm gold highlights, natural fruit colors.
- **Mood:** Quiet confidence, real light, real hands, real bottles. Avoid overly filtered “wellness influencer” looks.
- **Composition:** Generous negative space, centered or rule-of-thirds product, consistent crop ratios.
- **Typography on graphics:** Playfair Display for headlines, clean sans for body (or system).

---

## Content pillars (rotate weekly)

1. **Product** — Bottle shots, pour shots, ingredient close-ups.  
2. **Process** — Press, washing, labeling, early morning prep.  
3. **People / Place** — Customers (with permission), markets, St. Pete moments.  
4. **Education** — Short why cold-pressed, ingredient benefits (honest, not medical claims).  
5. **Behind the scenes** — Real work, not staged perfection.

---

## Post templates (quick reference)

- **Carousel:** 1 hero product + 2–3 detail/ingredient slides + final CTA.  
- **Reel:** 7–15 sec. Pour, press, or “day in the life” with simple text overlay.  
- **Story:** Daily special, location, poll (“Green Machine or Golden Hour?”).

Caption style: Short, direct, one clear sentence + light CTA. No emoji spam.

---

## Highlight covers

Create simple glass-style circular covers:
- Dark background
- Single icon or fruit emoji + short label
- Consistent stroke/weight

---

## Posting cadence (realistic for a working juice business)

- 3–5 feed posts per week maximum
- Stories most operating days
- Reels 1–2 per week when capacity allows

Quality > quantity. A real business does not need to post every day to win.
